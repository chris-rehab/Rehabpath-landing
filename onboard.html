Before we begin

Please read this carefully. We believe you should know exactly how your information is used.

What we collect: Your name, WhatsApp number, injury context, and daily exercise responses.

How it's stored: In an encrypted database hosted in AWS Sydney, New Zealand. Only your physio and RehabPath have access.

What we use it for: To send you timely, personalised support messages and to help your physio understand your progress.

Your rights: You can request access to your data or ask for it to be deleted at any time by emailing privacy@rehabpath.co.nz.

Sharing: Your data is not sold or shared with third parties. Anonymous, aggregated data may be used for research purposes.

By clicking "I agree", you consent to RehabPath collecting and using your information as described above, in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.
